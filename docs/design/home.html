Home (Today)

Home is the Today tab. It answers "how much can I still eat today" and gives one-tap access to the three things logged most: food, weight and cardio. Sample day: Thursday, Nov 12, 2026.

2,138 + 383 - 1,266 = 1,255 left. Food 1,266 = breakfast 490 + lunch 776.

Protein 86 g (20 + 66) and carbs 120 g (34 + 86) are the two meals added up. Fat is worked out from every item: 48.5 g, shown as 49 (the meals alone round to 30 and 18).

  • Tap the budget card: Summary for today.
  • Tap a meal: Food entry opens with the meal's saved entries as added lines and an empty text box, so lines that are not touched never change (food-entry.html, frame 13). Delete is in its menu, with Undo.
  • Tap the run: the cardio screen with its values, to edit.
  • Tap the weight row: the Weight tab (history and trend).

Meals with nothing logged stay listed, so a meal is always one tap away.

While a workout is running, a bar "Workout running, 23 min. Return" sits above the three buttons.

Text scales: at large text sizes the meal rows grow taller and the page scrolls. Nothing is cut off.

Unfolded, the same single column is used. Content stops growing at 600 dp and is centered. Bars and the tab bar stretch edge to edge.

No two-pane tablet layout. Folding or unfolding keeps the screen and any text being typed, because the layout is the same widget tree at a different width.

Each page has links at the top to redraw every frame at 344 or 673 dp.
