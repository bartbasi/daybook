Daybook design / Goals

Goals

Reached from More, then Goals. Sample person: male, born May 14, 1982 (44), 5 ft 11 in, 181.8 lb, lightly active, losing 0.5 lb a week.

Frame width: 360 dp (phone) 344 dp (Z Fold 5 cover screen) 673 dp (Z Fold 5 unfolded)
Decision for Bart: when to show the low-intake warning, and its words. Chosen: when the goal in use (calculated or your own number) is under 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 for women, a common guideline. One plain fact under the number, with no advice: "This is under 1,500 kcal a day, which is very low." (1,200 for women.) No blocking, no extra tap. Rejected: a warning based on the weekly rate (more than 1% of body weight a week).
Decision for Bart: rate options. Chosen: 0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 lb a week (0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 kg in metric), using 3,500 kcal per lb (7,700 per kg). Rejected: a free number box.
Ruled: the calculated goal is stored when Goals is saved. It stays the same until the user saves Goals again or taps Recalculate, so past days truly keep the goal they had. A quiet line says when it was worked out. Rejected: working the goal out again each day from the latest weigh-in. The number is kept in the existing goals row; see docs/phase-3-summary.md.

One scrolling screen. The result is at the top, with the working shown, and updates as any input below changes.

10 x 82.463 kg + 6.25 x 180.34 cm - 5 x 44 + 5 = 1,736.8, shown as 1,737. 1,737 x 1.375 = 2,388. 2,388 - 250 = 2,138.

Activity asks the user not to count workouts logged in Daybook, because those are added each day by the add-back setting.

Weight is not typed here. It comes from weigh-ins, so there is only one place to keep it right.

The number is stored in the goals row when Goals is saved: "Calculated Nov 8". It does not drift as weight changes. Recalculate works it out again from the latest weigh-in and saves it from today; past days keep theirs.

First run: Home shows a "Set your goals" card instead of a budget until Goals is saved once.
