Daily and weekly summary

Totals against goals for a day or a week, and which foods gave the most of each nutrient. Sample: Thu, Nov 12 at 3:12 pm, after breakfast, lunch and a run.

Frame width: 360 dp (phone) 344 dp (Z Fold 5 cover screen) 673 dp (Z Fold 5 unfolded)
Decision for Bart: goals for nutrients other than calories and macros. Chosen: start from the US Daily Values for adults (for example sodium 2,300 mg, fiber 28 g, saturated fat 20 g, cholesterol 300 mg), each changeable on the Goals screen. Nutrients with no Daily Value (sugar, trans fat) show "no goal". Rejected: no goals until the user sets them, which leaves most bars empty.
Decision for Bart: what a week is, and what an average is over. Chosen: Monday to Sunday, the same as weekly training volume; every average (calories and nutrients alike) is over the days with food logged. Rejected: the last 7 days ending today; dividing by 7 even when a day has no food logged, which makes a forgotten day look like fasting.
