Daybook wireframes, Phase 3

Grayscale boxes and text: layout and behavior only, no visual design. Each page shows a screen's important states side by side, with notes. Frames are drawn at 360 dp wide, 1 CSS pixel per dp. Every page has links to redraw its frames at 344 dp (the Z Fold 5 cover screen) and 673 dp (unfolded).

Sample data is one day, Thursday, Nov 12, 2026: a man of 44, 5 ft 11 in, 181.8 lb, losing 0.5 lb a week, goal 2,138 kcal. Numbers on each screen add up; food values are close to USDA values but are samples.

Tap counts for the six speed targets: speed-targets.md (plain text). The same counts are on the screen pages.

Navigation

Bottom bar, five tabs, each with an icon and a word: Today (Home), Summary, Workouts, Weight, More.

One tap from Home: Food entry (+ Food), the weight sheet (Weight), the cardio screen (Cardio), any meal or exercise logged today (to edit it), today's Summary (the budget card), and each tab.

Two taps from Home: log a saved meal or "same as yesterday" (+ Food, then the chip); start yesterday's or last week's workout, or any routine (Workouts, then Start).

Under More: things set up once and changed rarely: Goals, Saved meals and aliases, My foods and recipes, Exercises, Settings, About.

Foldables: one column everywhere. Nothing needs more than 344 dp. Unfolded, content stops at 600 dp wide and is centered; bars stretch. No separate tablet layout.

  5. Exercise detail and weekly volume

Rules used on every screen

  • Tap targets are at least 48 by 48 dp.
  • States are shown with words and shapes (labels, dashed or thick borders, hatching), never by color alone.
  • Text sizes are in sp and scale with the phone's setting. Rows grow taller and screens scroll; nothing is cut off.
  • Every save is written to the database at once and confirmed from what was read back. Deletes offer Undo instead of asking "are you sure". The only confirmation in the app is Import, which replaces all data.
  • No ads, no accounts, no ratings prompts, no donation prompts. Buy Me a Coffee is one row in About.
